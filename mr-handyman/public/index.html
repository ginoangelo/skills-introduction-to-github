Questions

How accurate is the diagnosis?

Mr. Handyman is very good at common repairs: plumbing, drywall, doors, fixtures, appliances. It tells you its confidence level and asks follow-up questions when photos aren't clear. It's guidance, not a guarantee. When in doubt, a pro should inspect.

Will it tell me to do something dangerous?

No. Gas, panel work, structural issues, roofing at height, asbestos/lead and large mold areas are always routed to a licensed professional, and emergencies get "do this right now" instructions.

Where do the prices come from?

Estimates are based on typical big-box store prices and regional labor rates. Add your ZIP code for a local estimate. Always confirm prices in the store and get quotes from pros.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are sent securely to our AI provider to create your report and aren't stored on our servers. Your repair history is stored only on your device.
