Questions

Is Aimed creepy?

No. It only reads the screenshot you choose to share. It can't see anyone's account, it doesn't keep your screenshot, and it never labels the person who posted.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app you can screenshot: stories, posts, captions, comments, and replies. Share the screenshot to Aimed from your photos or straight from the share sheet.

Is the read always right?

No app can know for sure. Aimed gives you a read with the evidence behind it, and it tells you when there isn't enough to tell. Adding one line of context makes it sharper.
