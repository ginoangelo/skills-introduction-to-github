# Aimed Instagram captions

Ready-to-paste captions for every Aimed post. Copy everything inside a box and paste it into Instagram. Each caption has 5 hashtags or fewer, because Instagram limits hashtags per post.

Before launch, change "free to try. link in bio" to "join the waitlist. link in bio" in every caption.
