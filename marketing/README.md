# Aimed on TikTok

How to research, make, and test TikTok videos for Aimed. Paid ads live next to this file: the still ads are in [`ads/`](ads/) and their 15-second scripts are in [`ad-scripts.md`](ad-scripts.md). Prompts for realistic AI clips in Google AI Studio are in [`ai-video-prompts.md`](ai-video-prompts.md).

**The one-line pitch.** Aimed is an app that reads between the lines on social media. Screenshot a post that's bugging you, and it tells you what it really means, whether it's probably about you, and why.

## Rules for every video

1. **Only use made-up posts.** Make them in Canva or with the Aimed example style. The one exception is someone describing their own situation with permission, and even then, blur every name and face.
2. **Never decode a real person's post in public.** That protects you legally and keeps the brand true to "reads posts, not people."
3. **No platform logos.** Say "a story" or "a post." Don't recreate Instagram, TikTok, or Threads designs.
4. **Label AI content.** Turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label for any video with an AI voice, avatar, or realistic AI footage.
5. **Promise only what's true.** Aimed gives a read with evidence, not a fact. Don't say "100% accurate."
6. **End with the call to action.** "Link in bio to get early access." After launch, use "Try it free."

## Step 1: Research before you post

Search these on TikTok, then read the comments. The comments tell you how people talk about the problem.

**The core problem**

- is this about me
- posting about me
- vague posting
- cryptic post
- passive aggressive post
- subliminals (Gen Z's word for indirect disses)
- sneak diss
- throwing shade
- subtweet

**Relationships, where the anxiety is highest**

- my ex posted
- did he post about me
- situationship
- soft launch / hard launch
- posting for him / posting for her
- close friends story
- song lyrics caption meaning

**How people solve it today, which is your competition**

- asked ChatGPT about my texts
- group chat decides
- overthinking
- analyze my texts
- what does this text mean

**Mood words that travel well**

- delulu
- overthinker
- read between the lines
- the group chat

**What to write down while you research**

| Listen for | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Exact phrases like "this is so me" or "why do I always think it's about me" | These become your hooks, captions, and App Store keywords |
| "I need this," tagged friends, "what app is this" | Proof people want a tool like Aimed |
| How they handle it now: best friend, group chat, ChatGPT | What Aimed replaces, and what to compare against |
| Which situation gets the most comments: exes, friends, coworkers, family | Where to make the most videos |

**Free research tools**

- **TikTok search bar autocomplete:** shows what people actually type.
- **"Others searched for"** under search results: related searches.
- **TikTok Creative Center:** trending hashtags, sounds, and top ads.

## Step 2: Tools

Check prices when you sign up. AI tools change fast.

| Job | Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideas, scripts, captions, hashtags | Claude or ChatGPT | Claude can write every script in this file word for word |
| Trends and sounds | TikTok Creative Center | Free |
| Editing | CapCut | Free auto captions, templates, background removal |
| Quick edits, green screen | TikTok's built-in editor | Good for reacting over a post |
| Made-up posts, thumbnails, carousels | Canva | Also has AI image tools |
| AI backgrounds for made-up posts | Adobe Firefly or Midjourney | For story backgrounds, not real people |
| AI voiceover | ElevenLabs, or CapCut text-to-speech (free) | Label AI voices |
| AI presenter | HeyGen or Captions | Use sparingly. Real faces usually perform better |
| Short mood clips | Runway, Pika, or Sora | For B-roll like a phone lighting up at 3 a.m. |
| Scheduling and cross-posting | TikTok's scheduler, Buffer, or Later | Post the same video to Reels and Shorts too |
| The real demo | Screen recording of Aimed | Your best content, because it's real |

**Starter setup that costs almost nothing:** Claude for scripts, Canva for the made-up posts, a screen recording of Aimed decoding them, CapCut to edit and caption, and your own voice or face.

## Step 4: Rollout

| Week | Post | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Videos 1, 2, 7, and 8 | Learn fast which angle people respond to |
| 2 | Turn the week 1 winner into a series, and add 3, 4, and 9 | Build a repeatable format |
| 3 and on | One video a day if you can, mixing the series with 5, 6, 10, and 11 | Grow the waitlist and test new angles |

- Test 2 or 3 opening lines for the same idea. The first 2 seconds decide most of a video's reach.
- Reply to comments with videos (number 10). It's free content and shows people you're listening.
- Cross-post everything to Reels and Shorts (number 12).

## Step 5: What to measure

| Measure | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shares and saves | People sending it to a friend is the strongest signal | TikTok analytics |
| "What app is this" comments | Direct demand | Comments |
| Average watch time | Shows whether the opening line holds people | TikTok analytics |
| Waitlist signups per week | The number that matters before launch | Your waitlist tool |
| Installs and trial starts | The number that matters after launch | App Store Connect, Google Play Console, RevenueCat |

Views alone don't matter much. A video with fewer views but lots of shares and "what app is this" comments is the one to make more of.
